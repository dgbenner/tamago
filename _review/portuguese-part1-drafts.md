# Brazilian Portuguese, A1 Part 1: drafted sentences

November 14, 2026. Every Portuguese sentence in Part 1 that is not from Tatoeba (172 of 819), for Fable's review and then a native speaker of Portuguese as spoken in Brazil. Each was written because Tatoeba had too few checked sentences for a word, or to give a question in the circle a reply for Answer the Question (marked reply). Each uses only words taught by its circle. Tatoeba sentences are not listed. Possible Tatoeba replacements are in portuguese-part1-candidates.md.

Two things to read for besides the usual: Brazilian usage (você, tem for there is, estar com fome, gostar de), and the Tatoeba lines themselves, since Tatoeba mixes Brazilian and European Portuguese under one language.
