# French, A1 Part 2: Tatoeba sentences kept out only by the trust rule

November 10, 2026. Each sentence below fits its unit on every other rule the pull applies (every word known by then or a listed name, 2 to 7 words, no digits, articles before a noun or adjective, linked English that carries the unit's nouns, not already in the course, not turned down in review). The only thing missing is the trust signal: its owner hasn't declared French native, and it has no OK tag. Nothing has been swapped.

**In all:** 217 candidates across the units. Of the 96 drafts, 5 are word for word on Tatoeba from an unchecked owner, and 33 have a candidate that teaches all the same new words.

Under each unit: its drafts, each beside the candidates that could replace it: the same sentence on Tatoeba first, then up to three that teach all the draft's new words; then the unit's other fitting candidates. These haven't had my review for idioms or odd lines; they pass the rules only. Links go to Tatoeba, where the owner and any comments can be checked.

**Review, November 10, 2026.** Only the candidates listed beside a draft were judged (the 5 exact matches and the rest), each for grammar, how it sounds to a French speaker, A1 fit and a true match with its English, and marked **Use** or **Reject** with a reason; the "Other candidates" lists below each unit are unreviewed. Marks are keyed to the Tatoeba sentence, so a sentence beside several drafts carries the same mark each time. Of the 39 distinct sentences beside drafts, 27 are Use and 12 are Reject; 6 Use marks name the English to show instead of Tatoeba's line. Of the 33 drafts that had a candidate, 26 now have at least one usable replacement; S11161, S11162, S10761, S10763, S10770, S10771, S10772 lost every candidate they had. Nothing has been swapped.
