# Italian, A1 Part 4: drafted sentences

November 16, 2026. Every Italian sentence in Part 4 that is not from Tatoeba (453 of 970), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Italian as spoken in Italy; the five vocabulary circles carry the Kelly list's formal and abstract words (U91 to U95: comma, decreto, provvedimento, ente, and verbs in the lui form such as effettua, dispone, rivolge), so read them for whether an A1 learner would meet them like this, and for stiff or odd lines; prices in euro; caro as dear in letters (U89); words in the sense their circle teaches (porta as door, perso as lost your way, entro as by).
