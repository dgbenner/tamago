# Italian, A1 Part 3: drafted sentences

November 16, 2026. Every Italian sentence in Part 3 that is not from Tatoeba (254 of 880), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Italian as spoken in Italy; times with e mezza, e un quarto, meno un quarto; months without capitals; the joined prepositions (del, nella, dal); the formal words of the news and the region circles (governo, stato, comune, regione), read for A1 sense; words in the sense their circle teaches (parte as leaves, aspetto as I wait, sicuro as sure).
