November 14, 2026. Each sentence below fits its unit on every other rule the pull applies (every word known by then or a listed name, 2 to 7 words, no digits, articles before a noun or adjective, linked English that carries the unit's nouns, not already in the course, not turned down in review). The only thing missing is the trust signal: its owner hasn't declared Spanish native, and it has no OK tag. Nothing has been swapped.

**In all:** 613 candidates across the units. Of the 138 drafts, 5 are word for word on Tatoeba from an unchecked owner, and 75 have a candidate that teaches all the same new words.

Under each unit: its drafts, each beside the candidates that could replace it: the same sentence on Tatoeba first, then up to three that teach all the draft's new words; then the unit's other fitting candidates. These haven't had my review for idioms or odd lines; they pass the rules only. Links go to Tatoeba, where the owner and any comments can be checked.

**Review, November 14, 2026.** Every candidate shown below (431 of the 613; the "and N more" tails are not in this file) was judged for grammar, how it sounds to a Latin American Spanish speaker, A1 fit and a true match with its English, and marked **Use** or **Reject**, with a reason for each Reject and the English to show where Tatoeba's line should not be used as is.
