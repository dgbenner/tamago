# German, A1 Part 2: Tatoeba sentences kept out only by the trust rule

November 14, 2026. Each sentence below fits its unit on every other rule the pull applies (every word known by then or a listed name, 2 to 7 words, no digits, articles before a noun or adjective, linked English that carries the unit's nouns, not already in the course, not turned down in review). The only thing missing is the trust signal: its owner hasn't declared German native, and it has no OK tag. Nothing has been swapped.

**In all:** 618 candidates across the units. Of the 191 drafts, 10 are word for word on Tatoeba from an unchecked owner, and 109 have a candidate that teaches all the same new words.

Under each unit: its drafts, each beside the candidates that could replace it: the same sentence on Tatoeba first, then up to three that teach all the draft's new words; then the unit's other fitting candidates. These haven't had my review for idioms or odd lines; they pass the rules only. Links go to Tatoeba, where the owner and any comments can be checked.

**Review, November 14, 2026.** Every candidate shown below (430 of the 618; the "and N more" tails are not in this file) was judged for grammar, how it sounds to a German speaker, A1 fit and a true match with its English, and marked **Use** or **Reject**, with a reason for each Reject and the English to show where Tatoeba's line should not be used as is. Meanings, genders and verb forms were checked against the course's Wiktionary data (content-de/checks). 330 Use, 100 Reject, 39 English picks. Nothing has been swapped.
