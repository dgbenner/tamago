# Romanian, A1 Part 1: Tatoeba sentences kept out only by the trust rule

November 21, 2026. Each sentence below fits its unit on every other rule the pull applies (every word known by then or a listed name, 2 to 7 words, no digits, articles before a noun or adjective, linked English that carries the unit's nouns, not already in the course, not turned down in review). The only thing missing is the trust signal: its owner hasn't declared Romanian native, and it has no OK tag. Nothing has been swapped.

**In all:** 184 candidates across the units. Of the 439 drafts, 10 are word for word on Tatoeba from an unchecked owner, and 92 have a candidate that teaches all the same new words.

Under each unit: its drafts, each beside the candidates that could replace it: the same sentence on Tatoeba first, then up to three that teach all the draft's new words; then the unit's other fitting candidates. These haven't had my review for idioms or odd lines; they pass the rules only. Links go to Tatoeba, where the owner and any comments can be checked.
