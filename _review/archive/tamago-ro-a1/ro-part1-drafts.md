# Romanian, A1 Part 1: drafted sentences

November 21, 2026. Every Romanian sentence in Part 1 that is not from Tatoeba (439 of 689), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Romanian Part 1 is 64% drafts (Tatoeba is dry at A1), so every line here matters: comma-below ș ț only; gender agreement (obosit/obosită, roșu/roșie, nou only with masculine and neuter nouns, since nouă is taught as nine); the enclitic article where Romanian needs it (mama mea, casa ta, câinele meu, ochii mari) and none after a bare preposition (pe masă, în bucătărie, la școală); tu only, no dumneavoastră; nominative-accusative only; set phrases (îmi place, mi-e foame, mă doare) as natives say them.
