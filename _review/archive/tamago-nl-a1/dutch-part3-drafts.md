# Dutch, A1 Part 3: drafted sentences

November 20, 2026. Every Dutch sentence in Part 3 that is not from Tatoeba (166 of 740), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Standard Netherlands Dutch. Read for: lines a Dutch speaker wouldn't say; untaught words; the polite u (taught in Part 2's U41) only in café, shop and official lines, je/jij elsewhere; het-lines (Het regent, Het is …) are drafts because the pull drops them.
