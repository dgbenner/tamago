> Appels: 2 euro per kilo  
> Markt: maandag en zaterdag

- How much is a kilo of apples? **2 euros** / 12 euros / 20 euros
- When is the market on? **Monday and Saturday** / Every day / Only on Sunday

**R102** (U47, notice) Markt op het plein

> Markt op het plein  
> Elke woensdag

- What can you buy there? **Fruit, vegetables, bread and cheese** / Clothes and shoes / Books and maps
