# Portuguese, A1 Part 4: drafted sentences

November 19, 2026. Every Portuguese sentence in Part 4 that is not from Tatoeba (269 of 812), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Brazilian phrasing throughout (no tu imperative, no European word order or vocabulary); grama is turned off and gramas carries the list word; ensino fundamental, médio and superior glossed as elementary school, high school and college; porquê only as the noun (o porquê).
