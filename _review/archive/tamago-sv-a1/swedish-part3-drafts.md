# Swedish, A1 Part 3: drafted sentences

November 21, 2026. Every Swedish sentence in Part 3 that is not from Tatoeba (313 of 980), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Swedish as said in Sweden, du throughout (no ni twins); times as Swedes say them (halv fyra is 3:30, kvart i tio); timetables on the 24-hour clock with a point (18.00); prices in kronor; senses: sen as late (not then), åt as the preposition (not ate), andra as second, tur as a turn, råd as afford (har råd med), låt as let, nå as the interjection Nå; definite forms only where taught (tåget, dagen, morgonen, helgen, sommaren, hösten, vintern, våren, framtiden)
