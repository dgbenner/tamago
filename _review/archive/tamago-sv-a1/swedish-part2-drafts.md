# Swedish, A1 Part 2: drafted sentences

November 20, 2026. Every Swedish sentence in Part 2 that is not from Tatoeba (308 of 845), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Read for natural, everyday rikssvenska with du throughout (no polite ni) and the meaning the English gives: kronor prices, ages with år, ha på sig for clothes, en/ett and the -t adjective form after det or an ett-word, inte after the verb, väl as a tag (Du kommer väl?), nog as probably; each line uses only words taught by its circle.
