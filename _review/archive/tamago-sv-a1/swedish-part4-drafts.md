# Swedish, A1 Part 4: drafted sentences

November 21, 2026. Every Swedish sentence in Part 4 that is not from Tatoeba (296 of 789), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Swedish as said in Sweden, du throughout (ni only as a plural, no ni twins); prices in kronor; no past tense (var as was cut), so no Vad hände?; senses: håll as direction (not the imperative of hålla), sida as side (not page), fara as danger, hälsa as greet (hälsa på, visit, cut), ring as the imperative of ringa (call), tag as a grip or a while, längre as further or longer, typ as type, andra still as second; definite forms only where taught (notan, hörnet, vägen, gatan, kartan, tunnelbanan); the vocabulary circles U91 to U95 carry the Kelly list's abstract words (draft-only ones turned off under the dry-source rule), so check each line sounds like something a Swede would say, not a gloss in a frame; bör and borde as should
