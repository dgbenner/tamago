# Polish, A1 Part 3: drafted sentences

November 21, 2026. Every Polish sentence in Part 3 that is not from Tatoeba (474 of 1293), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: case forms and tenses: the past (byłem, byłam, zrobił, poszła) from U71 on, with -łem said by a man and -łam by a woman; the future of być only (będzie from U72, będę from U64); the instrumental only from row 18 (z kolegą, jestem nauczycielem, jadę autobusem, pod stołem, interesuję się muzyką); no dative except the fixed podoba mi się and wydaje mi się; times as the feminine ordinal (jest ósma, o ósmej, wpół do czwartej, kwadrans po ósmej, za kwadrans dziesiąta); months in the locative (w maju) and seasons as w lecie, w zimie, na wiosnę, na jesieni; ty throughout; za in three senses (too, in, to the hour), worth a look
