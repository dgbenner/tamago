# Polish, A1 Part 1: drafted sentences

November 21, 2026. Every Polish sentence in Part 1 that is not from Tatoeba (242 of 841), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Polish case forms: each line should use only the nominative, the accusative for objects (piję kawę, mam psa, gram w tenisa, boli mnie) and the genitive only after nie with a masculine animal (nie mam psa) or as lat after a number; ty throughout (no Pan or Pani); gender agreement of adjectives with the speaker (zmęczony / zmęczona); natural word order; whether X to Y (Tom to student) reads naturally where jest would need the instrumental
