# Polish, A1 Part 2: drafted sentences

November 21, 2026. Every Polish sentence in Part 2 that is not from Tatoeba (463 of 1270), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: case forms: every genitive and locative is one the circle names (z Polski, w domu, butelka wody, nie ma chleba); no dative or instrumental; ty throughout; a perfective only after a modal or in a fixed phrase (miło cię poznać, poproszę, niech zobaczę); natural Polish word order; prices in złoty with złote after 2 to 4 and złotych from 5
