# Danish, A1 Part 3: drafted sentences

November 21, 2026. Every Danish sentence in Part 3 that is not from Tatoeba (187 of 893), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Danish as said in Denmark, du throughout (no De, I, jer); times as Danes say them (halv fire is 3:30), timetables on the 24-hour clock with a point; past and perfect forms (var, kom, har været) natural in context; each word in its taught sense (højere louder, ses as meet, sæt as put, lyst as wish)
