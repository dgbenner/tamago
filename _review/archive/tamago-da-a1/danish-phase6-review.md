## The judging themes, one by one

- **Introduced before tested, four a lesson.** Word audit 0 and 0 (2,006 lessons). Scope check: every live sentence within its circle's words except S66012 (verdict 9).
- **Accepted answers use taught words.** 4 of 11 generated en_to_fr answers pass (verdict 10); texts in verdict 11.
- **Gender, meaning, conjugation.** No article/noun mismatch in any live line. 109 nouns carry no gender: 94 are plurals and definite forms, 15 are the months, morgen, lov and kilo (verdict 3). 836 hints are Wiktionary's own text and 163 of those are form-of labels (verdict 1); Builder 2's 40 and the Part 2 to 4 definite-form hints ("the sun", "solen") are good. No tables by design.
- **Drafts.** 671 rows, all tamago_original, all within scope. Thin circles: Clothes U54 74%, Phone Numbers U42 54%, Forms U88 54%, Months U63 49% (Appendix F).
- **Idioms and odd lines.** Verdicts 4, 5, 7, 8. "Hvem tror du, du er?" (listening) is fine. The U75 **ked af det**, **glæder mig til**, **held** lines read fine under their hints.
- **Near-cognates.** Not handled (verdict 13); Danish has the most false friends on the board (barn, god, her, far, by, fire, to, time, sort, grim, have, man, hold, pas, mine, give).
- **Inflections in order.** Definite ending in U58, plural U06, comparatives U43/U44, -t and -e forms U57, past in U71 (Part 3) before any line uses it (Part 2 is clean), perfect in U73, imperatives U69 and U95, **da** (when) and **sin/sit/sine** in Part 4. Danish teaches the past earlier than Swedish because the frequency list leans on it; that is the right call and it is in order.
- **Register.** du throughout; hate and death lines are the only slips (verdicts 4, 5). **Hvad er dit køn?** on the form is right.
