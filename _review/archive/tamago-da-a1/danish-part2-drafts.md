# Danish, A1 Part 2: drafted sentences

November 21, 2026. Every Danish sentence in Part 2 that is not from Tatoeba (248 of 859), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Read each draft as a Danish speaker would hear it: natural modern Danish with du, numbers said the Danish way (halvtreds, femogfyrre; phone numbers in pairs), prices in kroner, have … på for wearing, and no line that leans on a word the circle has not taught yet (have as the verb is spelled like Part 1's have, garden, so the market lines ask with må jeg få and jeg tager).
