# Danish, A1 Part 4: drafted sentences

November 21, 2026. Every Danish sentence in Part 4 that is not from Tatoeba (126 of 672), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: Danish as said in Denmark, du throughout (no De, I, jer); prices in kroner, floors as anden sal; ordering through må jeg få and bede om (the verb have stays out); each word in its taught sense (ondt as pain, galt as wrong, slå op as look up, holde af as like, slap af as relax, pas på as look out, stykker in i stykker); the forms circle without postnummer, underskrift or udfyld (no Wiktionary entry)
