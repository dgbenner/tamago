# Portuguese, A1 Part 3: drafted sentences

November 16, 2026. Every Portuguese sentence in Part 3 that is not from Tatoeba (261 of 843), for Fable's review and then a native speaker. Each was written because Tatoeba had under three checked sentences for a word within the words known by that circle. Each uses only words taught by its circle or before it. The part's task items (Write the Number, Fill the Form, Read and Answer, Write a Message) follow; all of them are written for Tamago.

To read for besides the usual: European Portuguese phrasing (tenho de, tu forms and tu imperatives, estar a + infinitive), por que versus porque and por quê, como meaning like, accent-only pairs (esta/está, e/é, da/dá), natural Brazilian wording, a gente with the ele form
