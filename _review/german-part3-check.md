- Mock exam, Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1 (checked against the Goethe-Institut's Durchführungsbestimmungen): Hören about 20 minutes, Teil 1 to 3, 15 points; Lesen 25 minutes, Teil 1 to 3, 15 points; Schreiben 20 minutes, Teil 1 a form and Teil 2 a short text (about 30 words), written straight onto the answer sheet; Sprechen a group exam of up to four, 15 minutes, no preparation time, Teil 1 introduce yourself from keywords and then spell a word and say a number, Teil 2 ask and answer questions, Teil 3 make requests and respond to them. The recommended order is Hören, Lesen, Schreiben, then Sprechen. The format proposed in `_review/es-de-framework-needs.md` (listening, reading, writing with only a form and a message, speaking) matches. Two notes: (1) Sprechen Teil 1 ends with spelling a word and giving a number; if the course's speaking round can ask for one spelled word and one number, it would mirror the exam. (2) Hören Teil 1 and 3 are short dialogues and phone messages with three-option or true-false items, Teil 2 is public announcements (Durchsagen) with true-false items; the course's Write the Number items cover the numbers, times and prices those carry, which is the right A1 listening skill, and the Announcements circle (U68) feeds Teil 2 well.
