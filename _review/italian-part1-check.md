# Tamago Italian check, A1 Part 1, 2026-11-14

Quality pass on Italian A1 Part 1, change no code. Two sets: the Tatoeba candidates kept out only by the trust rule (126 distinct sentences in `_review/italian-part1-candidates.md`), each marked **Use** or **Reject**; and the 161 drafted sentences in `_review/italian-part1-drafts.md`, each marked **Keep**, **Fix** (with the corrected Italian and English) or **Cut**, with a swap named where a Use candidate teaches the same word. Use means natural, correct Italian as spoken in Italy, an accurate English, and right for A1. Meanings, genders and verb forms were checked against the course's Wiktionary data in content-it/checks (words.json, conjugations.json), not memory. The candidates file carries the same marks in place. This is a reviewer's read, not a native speaker's.

## Counts

- Candidates: **Use 97** · **Reject 29** (11 of the Use marks name the English to show).
- Drafts: **Keep 156** · **Fix 4** · **Cut 1**; 9 drafts have a swap named (1 is word for word on Tatoeba).
- Fixes: S5101 ("Ho un uccello" is the vulgar joke to an Italian ear; "L'uccello è piccolo"), S10108 ("una mia amica": amica keeps its article, unlike mia madre), S12109 (Mary inside an Italian sentence; Maria), S51113 ("sta bene" is for a person; of an arm, "va bene"). Cut: S51105 ("Che cosa hai in testa?" is "What are you thinking?").
- Reject reasons, most common first: Lei read as formal "you" where the learner knows lei as "she"; love lines and odd lines; idioms (that's why, suits you, "è di lei"); a register clash (ciao with "Come sta?"); sense traps (prego as "here you are", italiano as the nationality, capital Inglese); a relative clause; a two-speaker exchange; a fragment; one missing period.
- Accepted answers to widen: S3109 (Excuse me), S4105 (bevo il caffè), S12110 (figli).
- Nothing failed on grammar or spelling: articles (l'uomo, gli uomini, i ragazzi, le mele), agreements (stanca, nuova, rossa), the formal imperatives (ripeta, parli), c'è and ci sono, sul tavolo and in cucina are all right.
