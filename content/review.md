A sentence passes when its French is in Tatoeba exactly (ignoring curly apostrophes and spacing), a directly linked English translation matches the draft English or an accepted answer (I'm = I am), and the sentence is owned by a self-declared native French speaker or carries Tatoeba's OK tag.
