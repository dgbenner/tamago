# Design System Card

## What This Is

A demonstration of a design system for Tamago. It lives on its own branch, `design-system`, and is not part of the live app. Nothing here is merged into the app without Dan's say.

A design system is a shared set of parts (buttons, lists, sheets) with one agreed look, each shown on its own with every state it can be in. This Storybook is the place to see those parts outside the app.

## The Plan

- **Done: Tamago's current look.** The first set of components lifted out of the app's big files, looking and behaving exactly as they do in the app.
- **Done: iOS and Android styles.** Every component can also be drawn the iPhone way or the Android way, using Konsta UI where it has a matching control. Switch with **Style** in the toolbar; the **Styles** page explains how each one is made.
- **Done: two screens.** The Question Page and the Main Page, built only from these parts, in all three styles, with a Compare view that shows the three together. They are under **Screens** in the sidebar.

## What's in the Library

**Foundations** (the raw materials every part is made from)

- **Styles**: Tamago, iOS and Android, and how each component is made in each.
- **Colors**: every named color, in light and dark, and where it's used.
- **Type and Spacing**: the text sizes, gaps, corners and line widths the app uses.
- **Icons**: all 21 icons with their names.

**From the question page**

- **Button**: Check, Continue, and the quiet text link.
- **ProgressBar**: the bar at the top of a lesson.
- **QuestionHeading**: "Translate", "Pick the Meaning".
- **AudioHelp**: the play button with Slow and Show Words.
- **SentenceText**: the sentence with tappable word hints, and the new-word meaning under it.
- **ChoiceList**: the column of answer choices.
- **AnswerBar**: the verdict bar with Continue.

**From the main page**

- **CircleNode**: the lesson circle.
- **ToolTile**: a tool, as a pill or a card.
- **FlagRow**: the course flags.
- **Logo**: the wordmark and the sushi.
- **LevelLine**: the "Early A1" line across the tree.
- **Sheet**: the bottom sheet or pop-up.

**Added for the two screens**

- **TopBar**: the main page's header.
- **ToolStrip and ToolPanel**: the tools as a row (phone) and as a panel (desktop).
- **PinnedAction**: the pinned Next Lesson button.
- **IconButton**: a button that is just an icon.

**Screens**

- **Question Page**: Pick the Meaning, before answering, right, and wrong.
- **Main Page**: the header, the tools, part of the tree, and a circle's sheet open.
- **Compare**: each screen in Tamago, iOS and Android together, at phone size and at desktop size.

**Shared**

- **Toast**: the message that slides down from the top.

Stories are silent: nothing plays sound or calls the server.

## Where the Colors Come From

Every color is defined once, in `src/index.css`, as a named value, and the same file holds a second set for dark. Components never name a color directly; they use the names, so switching between light and dark changes everything at once. The **Colors** page lists them all.

## How to Use This Storybook

- **Style** (toolbar): Tamago, iOS or Android.
- **Theme** (toolbar): Light or Dark.
- **Size** (toolbar, the viewport button): Phone, 390 px wide, or Desktop, 1440 px wide.
- **Sidebar**: the screens first, then each component, with one entry per state.

The full list of components, with their settings and a usage example each, is in `COMPONENTS.md` at the top of the repository.
